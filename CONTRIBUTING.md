# Contributing Guidelines

We welcome contributions to improve Docker‑Link promotion materials and documentation.

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the repository** and clone your fork locally.
2. **Create a new branch** for your change, e.g. `feature/add-blog-post`.
3. **Add or modify files** under the `docs/` folder.
   - Markdown for blog posts should be placed in `docs/blog_posts/`.
   - Video scripts or links go to `docs/videos/`.
   - Assets (images, GIFs, logos) belong in `docs/assets/`.
4. **Run the CI workflow locally** (see `.github/workflows/ci.yml`) to ensure the Markdown renders correctly.
5. **Submit a Pull Request** targeting the `main` branch.
6. **Describe your PR** clearly: what you added/changed, why it matters for promotion, and any relevant links.

## Code of Conduct
- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Keep discussions constructive.
- No spam or self‑promotion unrelated to Docker‑Link.

## Review Process
- Maintainers will review within 3 business days.
- If changes are needed, reviewers will comment; please address them promptly.
- Once approved, the PR will be merged and the documentation will be published automatically.
